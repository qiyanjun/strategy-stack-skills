# Demo — Personal: end-to-end career analysis

**Prompt**

> I'm a tenured professor working on healthcare AI. Analyze my career strategically —
> am I defensible, and what should I do over the next two years?

**Which skill fires:** `personal-career-deep-dive`

**Chain it orchestrates** — deliberately mirrors the business track

| Phase | Skill invoked | Business-track counterpart |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `field-understanding` (target market) | `field-understanding` (industry) |
| 2 | *(inline)* labor market forces | Porter's Five Forces |
| 3 | `business-model-you` | `business-model-canvas` |
| 4 | *(inline)* peer landscape | competitive landscape |
| 5 | `personal-power-analysis` | 7 Powers |
| 6 | *(inline)* compensation reality | financial reality check |
| 7 | *(inline)* verdict + staged action plan | integrated verdict |

**What to check in the output**
- [ ] Powers are tested as *benefit + barrier* — a strength anyone can replicate is a commodity, not a power
- [ ] The AI-arbitrage test is applied: which strengths are being commoditized by AI?
- [ ] Peer comparison names real people or role archetypes, not abstractions
- [ ] The action plan is staged, with each action traced to a named gap

**The symmetry is the point:** same skeleton, different unit of analysis. A firm and a
career are both entities with a value proposition, competitors, and defensibility.
